Corporate siteFull-stack · 2026

IC Group

ic-group.ru

A holding of several businesses — retail, telecom, logistics, real estate — needed a corporate website with an interactive presentation of its structure and divisions, and easy content management.

  • Full-stack on Next.js 16 (React 19) + headless CMS Strapi 5 — content editable without a developer
  • Interactive 3D presence map on Three.js (React Three Fiber) with regions and markers
  • Dynamic news from CMS with filters by division and business
  • Animation with Motion: WebGL background, smooth scroll reveals
  • 3D performance tuning, responsive layout, deploy via Docker

Clinic websiteWordPress · 2026

Mister Smile

mister-smile.ru

A dental clinic needed a conversion-focused site from Figma mockups: services, doctors, online booking and patient trust — with self-service content management.

  • Custom WordPress theme built exactly to the Figma mockups — no page builders, clean markup
  • Content managed via ACF: services, doctors, pricing and contact pages
  • Online booking form sending requests to the clinic's mailbox (SMTP)
  • SEO with Yoast (meta, OG, schema.org), page cache, speed optimization
  • Accessibility mode for the visually impaired, live chat, mobile-responsive
